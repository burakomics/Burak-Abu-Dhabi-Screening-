About this demo

A proof of concept of Burak for preventive health screening, set up for the IFHAS age groups (18–39, 40–64, 65–75).

  • Illustrative data only. Every person, value and date is fictional. Nothing here describes a real person.
  • Reference ranges. Taken from the IFHAS reference sheet. Burak’s calculated scores and rule thresholds are illustrative until clinically signed off.
  • Decision support. Burak organises and flags results for review. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
  • Internal. Built for Burakomics review. Not to be shown outside without the programme’s permission.
